# Zenvocloud — Data Cleanup & Operations Automation Pilots

**Small, clear-scope projects for operations teams, inventory managers, and growing businesses.**

Zenvocloud is a developing digital-services project focused on practical data-quality and workflow prototypes. Our starting point is operational work: messy exports, inconsistent SKUs, repeated spreadsheet cleanup, and reports that need manual checking.

## First available service: CSV cleanup & data-quality review

**Indicative starter package: USD 89** (final scope and terms agreed before order).

- One UTF-8 CSV file with **up to 500 rows, 40 columns, 2 MB**.
- Normalize harmless whitespace and agreed SKU casing.
- Flag exact duplicates, missing values, and spreadsheet-formula-like entries in a separate review report.
- Deliver the cleaned file, a review list, and a concise quality summary.
- **No silent row deletion.** Originals are preserved.
- Prototype turnaround target: **two business days after valid inputs and agreed scope**, subject to availability.
- One revision of the agreed cleanup rules.

**Important:** The currently tested local CSV-processing lane works with UTF-8 CSV. Other file types, extra-large datasets, sensitive data, ERP access, recurring integrations, and hosting need a separate feasibility review. Do not upload sensitive or private data until data-processing terms are agreed.
