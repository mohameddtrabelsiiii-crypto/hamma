## Principle: profitable customers > store currency > trend screenshots

Keep every candidate and market in an evidence table. Price may be displayed in USD, EUR or any eligible checkout currency; accounting can normalize into USD or TND with the **rate timestamp** and account for conversion/spread, settlement currency, transaction fees, return liabilities and local tax obligations. Currency conversion cannot fix an unsupported merchant payout route. Never use fake foreign address, nominee account or other verification workaround.

**True per-order contribution =** collected product sale price (excluding any tax we remit) + shipping actually collected − actual supplier/base cost − transaction/payment/platform fees − shipping we fund − marketplace/FX charges − expected refunds, reprints, duties and customer-service reserves − paid acquisition cost. Record product currency, supplier currency, payout currency, exchange-rate date and region. Do not claim profit from displayed retail-price minus catalog base cost alone.

**Cash-flow gate:** Even a positive-margin third-party dropshipping product FAILS the owner's $0 constraint if a supplier charges our card, credit or deposit before customer payout clears. Prefer documented merchant-of-record/print-on-demand arrangements where real base cost is deducted from sale proceeds, with no upfront credit obligation. Confirm any exception with real terms.

## Product types to research — hypotheses, not proven winners

1. **Original art + niche identity:** Abstract desk/home sticker and mug art, tasteful hobby motifs, creator community merchandise. Fourthwall POD is first test channel. No unlicensed sports teams, celebrity likenesses, media franchise art or borrowed memes.
2. **Giftable meaningful designs:** Personalized name/date-free concepts, occasions and hobby micro-communities. Personalization must be genuinely supported by the fulfillment platform before offered.
3. **Functional home/desk accessories:** organization, productivity and everyday practical problems. For third-party sourcing, require traceable supplier and compliant safety claims; defer if supplier charges us before settlement.
4. **Pet-owner gifts:** original non-branded designs; do not make unsubstantiated medical or animal-safety claims. Pet photos only from authorized owners with clear usage rights.
5. **Seasonal and cultural interest:** evergreen core with cautious seasonal versions, no fabricated scarcity and no copyrighted personalities.

These are **test categories**. No niche is a winner until evidence of purchase intent, feasible fulfillment, acceptable unit economics and repeatable conversion supports it.

## Evidence-first product qualification

Per candidate capture: date, name, independent source URLs, target customer and problem, target market/country, evidence of current purchase intent (not just views), competition and differentiation, final product artwork, supplier location and terms, delivery by destination, customer support/returns, landed cost, platform+payment fees, cash-flow funding requirement, expected margin range with known assumptions, IP/safety/legal risks, organic traffic channel, and actual live test results if authorized.

**Pass/fail gates (all compulsory):**
- An original/differentiated sellable item; clear authorized intellectual property and appropriate accurate marketing.
- An actually connected and supportable supplier fulfilling target destinations, with documented quality/returns/shipping assumptions.
- Payout processor eligibility based on truthful merchant residence and bank details.
- Price/fees/FX verified, with positive conservatively modeled contribution and no upfront funding requirement.
- No deceptive health claims, fake reviews, artificial scarcity, environmental claims without evidence, unsafe/regulated products, counterfeits, or invented demand figures.
- A working checkout and a reachable real customer-support channel before public publication.

**Score only after gates pass** each dimension 0–5: purchase-intent evidence 25%, differentiation/rights 20%, after-fee margin 20%, supplier+shipping reliability 15%, organic visual demonstrability 10%, refund/support simplicity 10%. Weighted 0–100; tie-break by cleanest no-spend route and repeatable operational proof. A high score does NOT override hard gates.

## Free validation and customer acquisition pipeline

- Research buyer problems/questions and **dated** active demand through public search, Google Trends, Reddit discussions, marketplace search result pages, supplier catalogs and competitor review themes. Record sources and make no unsupported multi-million-view claims. Use searches to form hypotheses, not to scrape restricted sites, plagiarize sellers or spam communities.
- Create original product artwork and useful mobile-first listing photographs/mockups *only if authorized and accurate*; make clear they are renders where relevant. Evaluate how legible an image is at actual print size.
- Draft short-form demonstrations, behind-the-design clips, comparisons, gift guides and SEO pages for the target shopper's use case. Post only to channels for which legitimate access and posting permission exist; follow platform rules; do not insert commercial offers into unrelated communities.
- After lawful store launch, eligible sellers can seek **Google Merchant Center free listings** (not guaranteed placement) by providing true shipping/return policy, price, availability and quality product data. Google says setup and listings are free; check country/store eligibility at the actual application.
- Track: impressions, qualified clicks, product-view-to-cart, checkout initiation, confirmed purchases, gross sales, contribution after returns, complaints, and repeat/referral conversions. Record zeros and unknowns accurately; zero-sales testing is not revenue.
- Only repeat winners that actually satisfy real customer demand and contribution. Retire losing hypotheses rather than proliferating placeholder listings.

## Nine-specialist Cavalry implementation and QA

- **Atlas:** produce 3 dated, source-linked niche hypotheses, distinguishing opinions from observed buyer signals.
- **Muse:** generate one original, safe product idea with ownership/rights notes and physical design guidance.
- **Forge:** draft a SKU listing *only* with known specs; never claim a hidden idea exists in a real store.
- **Ledger:** assemble actual base, fulfillment, FX, payment and cash-flow figures; mark absent inputs UNKNOWN; block imagined positive margins.
- **Sentinel:** independently reject copyrighted art, fake supplier claims, unverified health claims, foreign KYC workarounds, fabricated demand, unsafe merchandise and off-brand hallucinations.
- **Pulse:** draft one lawful organic hook and creative brief (not a posted campaign).
- **Beacon:** produce truthful SEO title, meta description and free-listing eligibility checklist.
- **Harbor:** check supplier returns, support policy, delivery time and complaints resolution.
- **Relay:** check cron/API uptime, dated shop state and audit handoffs; do not confuse internal chat with external results.

**Automatic advancement condition:** A specialist may move a candidate to a *concept* or *artwork* stage in GitHub, but cannot publish, collect funds, message customers or change payment configuration without actual integrated permissions and validation. Keep verified vs draft flags at all times.
