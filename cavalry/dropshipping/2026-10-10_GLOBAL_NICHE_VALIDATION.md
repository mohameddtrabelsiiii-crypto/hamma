## Ranked research sprint: feasibility rank, NOT sales prediction

### 1. Journal / analog-ritual original stickers — RESEARCH FIRST

**Buyer:** Someone customizing a journal, reading log or scrapbook (first US/EU interest probe). **Why now:** Etsy spring 2026 measured rising journal-related queries; consumer use overlaps our existing original sticker artwork. But journal search growth does not specifically validate stickers.

**Product hypothesis:** A single distinct original orbit/arc illustration or *artfully designed journaling micro-motif* on an existing Fourthwall kiss-cut sticker blank. Don't offer a sticker sheet, personalization, waterproof use or dimensions without confirming this exact blank supports it.

**Differentiation test:** Artwork readable at physically small size; styled original imagery near a journal; clear use/quality caveat. Organic short content concept: a macro crop of the design with a paper journal, caption “A small circle of color for everyday notes” — use original licensed footage, no unverified physical product photography.

**Gate:** Fourthwall unit cost + payment fee, shipping to US/EU, small-size print legibility, processor verified. Even inexpensive stickers can suffer from disproportionately high flat fees and shipping.

### 2. Original geometric wall prints — RESEARCH SECOND

**Buyer:** Apartment desk/corner decorator, gallery wall maker, art gift shopper. **Evidence:** Etsy's abstract art +38% and gallery prints +80% searches (historic and directional). **Supplier gap:** No verified Fourthwall wall print has been configured. Research exact print size options, production regions, damaged-print replacement policy and shipping before deciding.

**Proposed differentiator:** A coordinated 2–3-piece concept using our own Arc Study geometry, with technically tested proportions and matching visual palette. DO NOT advertise bundles or gallery sets without actual provider support.

**Gate:** Real print blank, dimension, print QA, cost including shipping and damage risk. This is not a catalog listing yet.

### 3. Original art mug / desk gift — MAINTAIN and improve existing

**Already configured:** Night Geometry 11 oz Fourthwall HIDDEN. **Buyer hypothesis:** Visual desk accessory and modest art gift, with use story. **Evidence:** Etsy's “little treats” theme favors small meaningful gifts, but not a mug-specific increase. **Gate:** real manufacturing cost, processing, shipping and packaging breakage/returns. Do not claim a verified gift-ready box or delivery window.

### 4. Original geometric tee — MAINTAIN, not expansion first

**Already configured:** Contour Flow Fourthwall HIDDEN, multiple real apparel variants. **Risk:** additional print-size and fit QA, sizing returns, larger shopping consideration, higher shipping, competing tees. **Gate:** true margin and print quality per sizes/colors and real returns policy. Current storefront not ready for selling.

**Pet-owner / teacher / humor novelty items:** park as UNSCORED hypotheses until dated first-party or credible third-party purchase-intent evidence for a precise buyer sub-niche, distinctive original artwork, fulfillment compliance and true margins. Do not copy competitors' slogans or start dozens of speculative SKUs.
