## Strict order of execution
1. **Payout activation gate** (highest priority): obtain current read-only payout status. Fourthwall Settings > Billing and payouts > Set up your payouts, legitimate Tunisia Stripe Connect flow. Only rightful owner handles bank, identity, OTP/2FA and legal declarations. Stay COMING_SOON and report specific roadblock until verified ACTIVE.
2. **Commercial QA:** inspect actual shirt contrast, mug wrap, sticker legibility, variants and production specs; confirm original artwork rights. Ledger records Fourthwall production, platform/payment costs, seller-borne shipping, returns reserve, taxes and **net contribution per variant**. Verify real shipping support and return/contact policies in target markets. No zero-cost assumptions for unknown costs.
3. **Prelaunch audience research and content:** Atlas researches three audience angles from current public trend/search/competitor signals, time-stamps each. Muse prepares 3 original process/visual concepts; Forge drafts truthful previews; Pulse queues 7 engaging non-sales design/process posts. No publication or unsolicited DMs without channel authorization and compliant assets; do not market hidden items as purchasable.
4. **After checkout is legally and technically ready:** Publish only proven sellable offers. Launch controlled organic content on **Pinterest + Instagram Reels**, repurpose the same legally owned material to Shorts, and link to the appropriate real product page via allowed clickable surface. YouTube Shorts descriptions/comments do **not** offer normal clickable external URLs; put the shop link in channel profile. One audience and 2 channels first, not 8 empty accounts.
5. **Built-in retention/measurement:** Fourthwall includes cart recovery email reminders (default 3) and shop analytics with traffic source, orders, AOV, conversions, profits and UTM attribution. Confirm actual settings after launch and respect opt-outs. Offer free accounts/public posts as an optional community channel only if account/feature active and lawful consent preserved. Fourthwall auto-generates sitemap; Google Search Console submission when public and verified.
6. **After first verified profit:** Test 3 to 5 opt-in microcreator relationships who genuinely fit the aesthetic. Fourthwall Affiliate Plus pays STORE OWNERS for referring other creators; it is NOT a commission platform for people promoting our merchandise. If introducing creators with commissions, track unique promo codes and handle settlements only from verified sale proceeds, net of returns; no upfront creator fees or promises.
7. **Optimization cadence:** weekly compare real funnel (impressions, sourced clicks, product sessions, cart adds, checkouts, paid orders, refunds, **net profit**). Treat missing values as null. Kill only ideas with sufficient actual evidence of low demand; expand one profitable product family before adding more.

## Suggested low-pressure 30-day experimental workload
- Days 1–7: clear payout/legal/policy/design blockers, audit costs, 3 audience hypotheses, 7 original content drafts, validate owned publishing permissions.
- Days 8–14: if and only if eligible for public launch, release catalog and test one core audience with a small batch of social posts; collect actual clicks.
- Days 15–30: refine the strongest hook/product page, recruit aligned opt-in creator partners where feasible, publish additional pieces only when profitable/unit-checked and permitted.
- **Not a revenue guarantee:** test metrics are diagnostic. No verified buyer or spending until merchant records prove it.
