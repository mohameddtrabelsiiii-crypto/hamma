## Draft customer-facing FAQs — use only after checking local law, actual checkout and shop contact

**How are items made?** Most Med Art products are printed or produced after an order is placed and fulfilled by Fourthwall's production partners. We do not maintain our own inventory.

**When will my order arrive?** Orders require production time before shipping. Fourthwall's published guidance generally estimates 2–5 business days for production, with shipping depending on the item, destination and carrier. Review the current delivery estimate at checkout; it is not a guaranteed delivery date.

**Do you ship internationally?** Availability depends on the product, supplier and destination. Enter your address at checkout to see shipping options. Duties and import taxes may be charged at checkout or on delivery depending on the destination and fulfillment route.

**Can I return or exchange a made-to-order product?** Fourthwall generally does not accept buyer-remorse or preferred-size returns for its made-to-order products. Eligible quality defects, fulfillment mistakes or wrong items may qualify for a replacement, subject to applicable consumer-protection laws and Fourthwall's current policies. Check fit and size before buying.

**My item arrived damaged or incorrectly printed. What should I do?** Contact the verified Med Art shop support channel as soon as possible and within Fourthwall's published 30-day reporting window, including the order reference and clear photos. Fourthwall's team reviews the issue with its production partner. Do not publish an invented support address.

**How can I track my package?** Fourthwall sends a shipping email with a tracking link after dispatch. Tracking updates may be delayed during carrier handoffs.

**Will the print match my screen exactly?** Colors and placement can look a little different across monitors, garments and actual production. We check artwork placement in the designer, but photographs and mockups are illustrative.
