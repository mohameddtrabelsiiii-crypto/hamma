## Admission rules

Every proposed winner must have buyer evidence, a distinctive and rights-safe product, verified supplier/fulfillment and return policies, legal processor payout, known fees and FX spread, acceptable contribution after customer service and return reserves, and $0 upfront cash-flow compatibility. If any answer is unknown, **do not score as ready**. Do not count drafted products as actual listings.

Where true, record links and dates for buyer evidence, delivered-country shipping quotes, real prices, transaction fees and merchant settlement. Research and organic promotion use only free lawful channels. We will choose customers worldwide rather than using store currency to decide geography.
