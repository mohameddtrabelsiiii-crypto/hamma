# Med Art — Zero-budget acquisition experiment queue

**Stage:** Prelaunch. This is a scheduling/content **draft**, not published marketing. No marketing claims of stock, order shipping, customer reviews, or availability until they are verified.

## Positioning hypothesis

**Med Art:** original geometric artwork printed on simple everyday merchandise. Three creative themes: Orbit Notes (circles, color, movement), Contour Flow (flowing abstract lines), Night Geometry (graphic blocks). Audience hypothesis: people who like minimalist design and giftable art. **Demand is not yet proven.**

Creative work must come from verified original artwork; keep production mockups honest. Do not represent digitally composited visualizations as customer photos or physical samples.

## Organic channels to test

1. **Pinterest:** inspirational compositions with original images, descriptive alt text and non-spam copy. Do not use unauthorized brand/competitor logos or scraped images.
2. **Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts:** process clips and design explanation using original artwork and music licensed for that use. Avoid fabricated product unboxings or fake UGC.
3. **Storefront SEO:** accurately name product type/design, write accessible descriptions, and link a real product page only once public and permitted.
4. **Permission-based community participation:** offer useful design discussion only where group rules allow, never mass-DM or auto-comment.

## Research before publication

For each candidate keyword or niche, collect **at least two dated public-source demand signals** and a comparison of competing product quality and pricing. Record URL, date, query, audience hypothesis, and what the evidence actually shows. Do not confuse a competitor's follower count with sales.

Working queries to validate: *minimalist geometric stickers*, *modern abstract mug design*, *abstract line art shirt*, *simple original art gifts*. These are **hypotheses**, not measured high-demand keywords.

## Launch-safe schedule

- **Before payout active:** design review, unpublished drafts, SEO copy, store policies, and research only. No claims of availability.
- **After payout + legitimate checkout verification:** publish accurate listings, carry out one organic test in one authenticated channel, use UTM tags where possible, and log results.
- **Weekly:** compare measured traffic, carts, sales and net contribution. Stop testing weak concepts and do not manufacture positive performance.
- **On any account/auth/payment block:** stop unsafe write actions, log problem and await required lawful provider flow. Do not bypass identity verification or switch merchant country.

## Tracking schema (no buyer PII)

For each eventual post: `date_utc, channel, asset_id, post_url, status, hook, audience_hypothesis, cost_usd, impressions, clicks, add_to_cart, orders, actual_net_contribution, source, verified_at`. Unobserved numbers are **null** rather than fabricated zeros.
