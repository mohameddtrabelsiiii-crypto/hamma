## Project description (copy)
Are duplicate leads, inconsistent columns, and broken CRM imports wasting your time?

I will clean and prepare CSV or Excel data you already own for a safer, clearer CRM import. My workflow preserves source identifiers and uses agreed, transparent rules to flag duplicates, missing information, inconsistent formatting and ambiguous rows instead of silently deleting records.

You'll receive a cleaned CSV, a concise list of changes and exceptions, and a reconciliation summary so you can check what happened to the original rows. I can align output columns with the import template you provide.

I also built a working browser-only CSV review tool as a technical demonstration: https://taskforge-ai.pages.dev/automation/leadops. It does not upload your file, access your CRM or send messages. **Please do not enter confidential customer data into public examples.** Your paid project will be handled only through agreed Upwork file sharing and your approved security requirements.

This service is data cleaning and validation, NOT verified lead generation, paid contact databases, email scraping, AI-enriched contact claims, or direct CRM deployment. Any uncertain matches will be marked for your approval. For custom API/CRM automations, request a separate quote.

## FAQ
**Will you delete duplicates?** Only exact duplicates per agreed rules may be removed when you explicitly approve that action; unclear matches remain flagged for review.
**Can you guarantee every phone/email is accurate?** No. I check format and provided-source consistency; I do not claim third-party verification without an agreed external source.
**Can you write directly to HubSpot, Salesforce or another CRM?** Not in these packages. I deliver an import-ready file; API integrations are separately scoped.
**Can you work with private customer data?** Only through approved handling under the platform contract. We minimize access and do not publish or reuse private datasets.
**Do you use AI?** Automated validation and rule-based cleanup where safe, with human QA for ambiguous records. We do not invent missing details.
