## On-platform truthful proposal drafts — customize after eligibility

### A1 Customer Information

I can map the Excel/CSV files to your new template, preserve source references, normalize agreed phone/date formats, and place uncertain duplicate matches in a separate review tab. I'll reconcile record totals and include an issue log. I have a working browser-only CSV triage demonstration at https://taskforge-ai.pages.dev/automation/leadops ; it demonstrates the existing prototype, not a completed client engagement. Could you share a redacted sample, approximate row count and the exact rule for treating two contacts as duplicates? I'd propose a fixed milestone once the scope is clear.

### A2 Small Excel
Hello — this fits a small, accurately scoped delivery. I will copy the supplied records into the requested headers, preserve original spelling and identifiers, and run a second pass for misplaced fields, missing values, duplicate-looking records and record-count reconciliation. I'd keep the output as an editable .xlsx with a short issue note. Can you share the number of source files and a redacted example row so I can give a firm fixed price and delivery time?

### A4 PDF PO extraction
Hello — the reliable part of this workflow is mapping each purchase order field to a checked Excel structure, then flagging PDFs that cannot be parsed confidently instead of silently creating bad orders. I'd begin with sample PO templates and agree the required checks (order number, date, customer, items and totals). If a Power Automate license or premium extraction connector is needed, you would need to provide or approve it; I won't assume free tools can process every scanned format. Are your PDFs all the same layout, and is the mailbox in Microsoft 365?

### B1 Purchase PDF
Hello — I can scope a focused generator that reads your workbook and produces one printable PDF per unique purchase bill number, containing the company/buyer fields, line items, dates and source totals. The workflow will check for missing bill numbers, conflicting totals and malformed rows and provide a reproducible script plus sample PDFs. Before bidding I would like a redacted sheet showing whether each order is one row or several item rows, and a target sample bill layout. I will not claim past delivered client cases; I can instead demonstrate the approach using fictional data.
