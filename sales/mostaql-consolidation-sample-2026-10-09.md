## What was demonstrated
Three fictional source workbook names (`branch_A.xlsx`, `branch_B.xlsx`, `branch_C.xlsx`) with distinct worksheet names, 12 illustrative entity records, and a single merged sheet.

The example includes:
- A **Merged Records** sheet with source filename, worksheet and exact source row for every record.
- QA formula identifying missing original IDs and possible duplicate IDs.
- A separate **Review Queue** for the two duplicate-looking rows and the one record with a missing ID.
- A **Source Audit** sheet reconciling the imported row count per source workbook/sheet with the merged output.
- A compact summary with imported rows, number requiring review, source count and reconciliation status.
- **Instructions** explaining limitations, scope and conditions for a real import.

Verified workbook-level example:
- 12 demonstration records
- 3 fictional files and 3 source sheets
- 3 records needing review (2 duplicate-like, 1 missing ID)
- Source reconciliation `MATCH` (all 12 rows present)
