Status: commercial positioning and internal sales scope; **not** a claim of certification, audited security, or completed customer work.

## Positioning
We help small businesses clean operational data, automate repetitive workflows, and improve the reliability and security of their cloud operations. Start with a bounded paid pilot, measurable acceptance criteria, and explicit customer approval for any live-system changes.

Deliver evidence-based findings and prioritized recommendations. No penetration testing, intrusive scans, guarantees of compliance, or security certification.

Prices are **hypotheses**; adjust after real customer conversations, effort measurement, and currency/payment-provider validation. Do not insert these prices into existing TND orders or enable checkout until payment configuration and settlement are proven.

## Qualification before quote
- Customer identity/contact and clear business problem
- Dataset/system ownership and explicit authorization
- Example inputs or synthetic samples without confidential data
- Required output, deadline, acceptance criteria, integrations, constraints
- Data sensitivity, retention/deletion needs, security boundaries
- Payment method supported and quote accepted before starting paid work

## Delivery and trust rules
- Never promise capabilities that have not been tested; never imply certifications or expert pentesting credentials.
- Never upload customer secrets, API keys, or private data to public forms or demos.
- Read-only access by default; least privilege and written authorization for any changes.
- Test on synthetic data; use a change plan, rollback, and customer signoff for production writes.
- Do not claim orders, leads, revenue, deployments, or successful payments without verification.
- Maintain $0 additional cash spend unless explicitly approved otherwise.
