## Method and content system: original value -> real click -> verified purchase
- Every Pin must have a truthful headline and a meaningful original 2:3 visual. Real article destinations must contain genuinely useful unique instructions and match the advertised topic. No click bait to unrelated storefront or generic home page.
- Format bank: room arrangement guide, 4-step visual checklist, gallery wall spacing diagram, 5 ways to style a desk, printable journal page, gift-wrapping technique, color palette selection with factual notes, split-screen labelled **concept visualization** (never fake real renovation), original stop-motion illustration, motion infographic, topical seasonal guide.
- Use appropriately licensed or own artwork only. No cloning viral Pins/video creators. No unrelated aesthetic pics monetized via deceptive outbound links.
- Build topical boards **after authenticated actual Pinterest board-write access**: Small Spaces & Decorating, Desk Setup Inspiration, Original Printable Wall Art, Journaling & Pen Pals, Seasonal DIY Décor. Only Pen Pal Printables board 1095008165590387148 is VERIFIED currently. Do NOT place car/home ideas into that board; Metricool doesn't provide a create-board action.
- Publishing test: first 14 days 1–3 distinct genuinely useful Pins/day where board and landing page match; subsequently 3–6/day if quality and outbound click data justify it, abiding by platform limits. No promises of 1,000/day, no paid scheduling services, no fake engagement. Pinterest says quality matters more than fixed quantity.
- Editorial library drafts, production QA and measurement can be high volume **offline**; only original checked pieces may enter actual provider queue. Do not claim unpublished drafts equal live Pins.
- Metrics each week from Metricool Pinterest: verified Pin URL, impressions, saves, pin clicks, **outbound clicks**, landing visits, signups/qualified leads, legitimate funded payments, refunds, contribution margin. Separate impressions from paid revenue. Record unobserved data as null.
- Eligible future monetization: approved, disclosed affiliate offers; own digital downloads only after checkout/delivery/banking verified; Fourthwall POD only after active payouts and SKU margins; optional display ads once site qualified. **Pinterest doesn't generally pay creators per view**.
- Account branding quality: current Metricool account default profile image and empty bio. Metricool provides content scheduling but no profile editing or board creation. Authenticate direct Pinterest UI for account profile improvements or use a supported official integration; don't imply changes done.
- Website claiming: Pinterest https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/claim-your-website explains verification with an account-issued HTML tag/file/TXT. Do not invent a Pinterest claim token or assert site claimed.
